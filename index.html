How to use

Watch the video
  • Open a property. Tap a pin on the map, or a photo below it.
  • See one kind. Tap Land, House, Flat, Room, Business or Shutter. “View all” has more filters: sale or rent, place, price, size, bedrooms and road width.
  • Save it. Tap the heart. Saved properties stay on this phone, under Saved.
  • Ask about it. Press Contact to call or send a message.
  • Go there. “Get directions” opens Google Maps at the exact spot.
